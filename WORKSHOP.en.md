# Edge Travel - Workshop EN

## Title

Edge Travel

## Short Description

🚧 Turns the edge of a RimWorld map into a path forward, not an invisible wall.

## Tags

- Gameplay
- World
- Colonists
- Utility
- Experimental

## Description

🚧 **Edge Travel** makes the edge of the map the beginning of a journey.

In vanilla RimWorld, a colony usually lives inside a single local map while nearby world tiles feel distant and separate. This mod explores a different approach: when a colonist reaches the edge of the map, they can move into a neighboring area and continue their journey there.

The idea is simple: a settlement should feel less like an isolated box and more like part of a larger world.

## What It Changes

- The edge of the map stops being an invisible wall.
- Neighboring areas become part of the colony's story.
- It creates a foundation for larger bases, border zones, and exploration around a settlement.
- Visited areas are saved and restored when you return.

## What Works Now

- Edge travel for drafted colonists.
- Creation of neighboring areas on adjacent world tiles.
- Saving and restoring visited maps.
- `Edge Travel` objects are visible on the world map.
- Automatic edge travel can be disabled in the mod settings.
- Debug tools only appear when developer mode is enabled.

## Important

⚠️ This is an early experimental version.

It is best tested on throwaway saves. Right now the mod is proving the foundation for a future "living map edge", not delivering a finished seamless-world system.

## Requirements

- RimWorld 1.6
- Harmony
